AI Protein Property Predictor

Can a computer tell if a protein is a membrane protein just by reading its letters?

17,342 human proteins21 simple features77.5% to 79% accuracyProject B21 | FAIR-AI-21

The idea in simple words

A protein is a word

It is written with a 20-letter alphabet called amino acids, for example MKLLVVIAL. The order of letters decides its shape and job.

The membrane is oily

The skin of a cell is oily. A protein living there needs oil-loving (hydrophobic) letters such as L, I, V and F, like a raincoat for standing in the rain.

How we built it

Which amino acids mattered?

V W L I F

Push towards membrane. Oil-loving letters that fit inside the oily membrane.

K H R D Q

Push towards not membrane. Charged or water-loving letters.

Direction comes from Logistic Regression. The model learned the same biology we expected.

Honest findings

Fair split check

Random split F1 = 0.720, family split F1 = 0.721. No real difference here, probably because 21 simple features cannot memorise proteins (we did not test this).

Limitations

Labels come from a text search and are noisy. Letter order is ignored. Human proteins only. Models were not tuned.
